Kimia Rafieibuzhani is a Master's student in Biotechnologies for Food Science 
at the University of Padua and is currently an intern in the PMIG group.

Coming from a wet-lab background, she is shifting toward computational 
biology and bioinformatics. Her Master’s thesis centers on genetic elements 
driving antimicrobial resistance (AMR), with a particular focus on long-read 
sequencing technologies for genomic analysis.

Outside the lab, you will likely find her testing pastries at her latest 
favorite café or picking up a few words of a new language.
