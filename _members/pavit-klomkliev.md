Hi, I'm Pavit (a.k.a. Jin), a visiting PhD researcher in the Precision 
Microbial Genomics group at HZI. I'm a PhD student in Bioinformatics and 
Computational Biology at Chulalongkorn University, Thailand.

My research focuses on microbiome analysis using metagenomic sequencing, 
bioinformatics, and machine learning. During my PhD, I study the gut 
microbiome of infants to identify microbial patterns associated with 
health and disease.

At the HZI, I will work on computational approaches to study the interaction
between mobile genetic elements and their bacterial hosts.

Outside of research, I enjoy jogging, going to the gym, drawing and painting, 
playing video games, exploring new places, and trying local food.
